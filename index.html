Pipeline details & hardware

NER + LLM (default): An ai4privacy multilingual NER model (~150 MB) detects 20+ PII types including names, emails, phones, addresses, IDs, dates, and more across 8 languages including Dutch. Then a Qwen2.5 LLM pass verifies and catches any remaining PII. Best accuracy.

LLM only: Qwen2.5 alone handles all entity detection. Faster to start, but may miss some entities.

  • 1.5B — ~2 GB VRAM. Runs on most modern laptops (integrated GPU).
  • 3B — ~4 GB VRAM. Needs a dedicated GPU or Apple Silicon Mac.
  • 7B — ~8 GB VRAM. Needs a dedicated GPU with ≥8 GB.

Requires WebGPU (Chrome/Edge 113+, Safari 18+). First load downloads models; subsequent runs use the browser cache.
